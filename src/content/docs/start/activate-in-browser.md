1. Open **dagny.m5bank.app** and choose **Start free**, or go to `/iam`.
2. Enter your name and choose your **state chain** (your own choice; it is not verified).
3. Optionally add an email or mobile number. It stays in your browser.
4. Read the statement, tick **"I am attesting to my humanity. I am a human being."** and press **Activate my IAM**.

You will see **"Your IAM is active."** with your private vault, your registry entry and your certificate.

## Where your IAM is kept

Only in your browser. M5 stores nothing. Use **Download my M5POD (.zip)** to keep a copy, and **Remove my IAM from this browser** to delete it there.

:::caution
Clearing your browser data removes the IAM from that browser. Download your M5POD first if you want to keep it.
:::

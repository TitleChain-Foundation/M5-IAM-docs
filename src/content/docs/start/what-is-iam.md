**IAM** (Identity and Access Management) is the free starting point for every person in M5. Account `M5IAM-000`, tier **M0**.

You create it by attesting to your humanity in one short statement and **accepting the CYRUS Protocol**. That gives you:

- an **IAM account**, active and free for life;
- your **attestation in three matching parts**: the words you read, the data software reads, and the check that confirms nothing changed;
- the protection of the **CYRUS Protocol**, and your **right to refuse consent**: selected by you, activated when your credentials are verified;
- a **simulated TitleChain Registry entry**, holding only a commitment hash;
- a **sample Certificate of Title** for your IAM origin;
- your own **M5POD**: a private vault, organized from your personal life to your business.

## The CYRUS Protocol

Accepting the CYRUS Protocol is required, because it is what protects you. It is held by the TitleChain Sovereign Purpose Trust, for humanity, and covers:

- **Basic rights:** no slavery, servitude or forced labour; freedom of thought, conscience and faith, any faith or none.
- **Sovereign rights:** identity, data, privacy, self-determination and portability.
- **The right to refuse consent**, which you use as situations arise.

On an IAM you can **select** your right to refuse consent, at activation or any time after. It shows as **Selected · not activated**, because an IAM is self-attested and your credentials are not verified yet. It **activates when your credentials are verified**, by registering Bank of Me or by an approved agency verifying your IAM through M5-x401.

## What IAM is not

- Not a TCID. No TitleChain ID is issued.
- Not a verified identity. Nobody has checked your statement.
- Not an M5 Bank account and not a licensed M5POD.
- It cannot authorize a payment, signature, title transfer or agent action.

Every registered account (Bank of Me through Bank of Government) is built on an IAM. See [Accounts](/M5-IAM-docs/accounts/overview/).

Later, an approved agency can verify your IAM and you can move to a sovereign ID without starting over. See [From IAM to sovereign ID](/M5-IAM-docs/accounts/from-iam-to-sovereign-id/).

## How the fees work

- Every account pays **$5 a year for its name**; the first year is waived when the account is activated.
- Bank of Me renews at **$54 a year** from year two ($49 + $5).
- Sub-accounts activate separately.
- **Secretary of State and other government fees are separate** and not included.

## People first

An entity account belongs to the entity, not to a person. Each person who operates it needs their own Bank of Me account first, and holds a role in the entity. People can come and go; the account stays with the entity.

See each account for a sample of what it includes.

## How the fees work

- Every account pays **$5 a year for its name**; the first year is waived when the account is activated.
- Bank of Me renews at **$54 a year** from year two ($49 + $5).
- **Everyone pays $49.** Every person who acts for an entity (owner, signer, agent, member) holds their own active Bank of Me. The entity's fee covers the entity, not its people.
- **One account per legal entity.** Each subsidiary, department, board or affiliate registers separately, and sub-accounts activate separately.
- **Fixed prices up to Bank of Business.** Institutions and government pay the core price; implementation, migration, dedicated support and volume are by custom quote.

## What $49 covers

Your reusable ID: verify once and reuse it everywhere. During the year your record is re-checked against its sources and monitored, so its status stays current. The year also includes your member block for a private agent, a registered M5POD, product licences and the [M5 protocols](/M5-IAM-docs/accounts/protocols/).

## Always separate

- Secretary of State, IRS, county, tribal and other government fees
- Outside verification (KYC, background checks, credential-issuer fees), billed at cost
- Optional cloud services and optional support

## People first

An entity account belongs to the entity, not to a person. Each person who operates it needs their own active Bank of Me account first, and holds a role in the entity. People can come and go; the account stays with the entity.

See each account for a sample of what it includes.

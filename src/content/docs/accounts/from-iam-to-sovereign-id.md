Your free IAM is a self-attested human root. It is built so that, later, an approved agency can verify you and you can move to a full sovereign ID, **without starting over**.

## Verification by an approved agency

Through **M5-x401**, an approved agency can confirm your identity and bind its credential to your IAM. This includes agencies that rebuild identity for people who have lost theirs, such as displaced and migratory people, through the United Nations or another approved body.

- Only agencies holding an approved M5-CV credential can verify.
- The agency's credential states exactly what it confirmed, and stays the agency's own signed claim.
- Your history stays with you, in your M5POD.

## Payments through an agency

Once verified, a person can be eligible for **M5Pay (M5-x402)** under that agency. Non-governmental organizations working with the agency can then pay **directly** to the IAM-registered human, with a receipt for every payment.

## Moving to Bank of Me

When you are ready, your IAM migrates to a **Bank of Me (M5BKOM)** account with a sovereign ID. Your IAM origin, attestation and ledger carry forward; nothing is reissued, and the protocols of a registered account switch on (see [Protocols included](/M5-IAM-docs/accounts/protocols/)).

## Signatures

Your IAM record reserves a place for signatures. At verification, it is designed to carry the issuing agency's signature over its claim and your own signature as holder. Software releases are signed separately; see [Signatures](/M5-IAM-docs/security/signatures/).

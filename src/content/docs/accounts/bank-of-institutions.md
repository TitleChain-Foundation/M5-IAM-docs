## How it is organized

- **By place:** nation, tribal nation or territory, state, county and city.
- **By industry:** each institution is classified by its NAICS code, for example 522110 Commercial Banking, 522130 Credit Unions or 524 Insurance Carriers. Outside North America, the UN ISIC standard applies.
- **People first:** the people who run it register as Bank of Me members, then secure the credentials to activate the institution.

Its folders follow the same areas as Bank of Business.

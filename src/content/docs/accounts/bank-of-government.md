## How it is organized

Each judicial, legislative and executive body is organized by its **place and jurisdiction**: nation, tribal nation or territory, state, county and city. It is classified under NAICS sector 92, Public Administration:

| Branch | NAICS |
| --- | --- |
| Judicial | 922110 Courts |
| Legislative | 921120 Legislative Bodies |
| Executive | 921110 Executive Offices |
| Combined | 921140 Executive and Legislative Offices, Combined |
| Tribal | 921150 American Indian and Alaska Native Tribal Governments |

## People first

People register first as Bank of Me humans, then secure their credentials to activate the agency. Sub-accounts activate separately. Every action needs human approval.

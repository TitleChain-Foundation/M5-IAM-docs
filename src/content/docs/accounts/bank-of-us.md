**For:** a family, co-op, nonprofit or trust · **SKU:** `M5BKOU-002` · **$79 a year** (pending launch), name $5 a year with the first year free.

## What you get

- An account that **belongs to the group**, with roles for the people who run it.
- Its own registry validation, namespace and **M5-CV** for the entity, with jurisdiction built in.
- The same ten vaults, organized for running the group.

## Your folders

The same areas as Bank of Business, with *Programs and members* in place of *Service and customers*, and *Giving and store* in place of *Sales and commerce*.

| Area | Folders |
| --- | --- |
| The entity | `identity/formation/`, `identity/registrations/` |
| People and authority | `authority/`, `credentials/team/`, `personal/hr/` |
| Product | `personal/products/`, `legal/ip/`, `credentials/software-titles/` |
| Production and media | `personal/production/`, `comms/publishing/` |
| Service and customers | `contracts/customers/`, `contracts/service/`, `contracts/` |
| Marketing and publishing | `comms/brand/`, `comms/marketing/`, `comms/publishing/` |
| Sales and commerce | `contracts/sales/`, `financial/accounts/` |

| Governance and legal | `governance/resolutions/`, `governance/audit/`, `legal/compliance/`, `legal/counsel/`, `legal/risk-insurance/` |

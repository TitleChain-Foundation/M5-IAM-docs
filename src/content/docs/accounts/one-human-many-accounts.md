One human has **one root identity** (M5HUM and TCID), held through the IAM and their own M5POD. That person may hold several accounts: Bank of Me for their own life, and roles in a co-op or a business.

- **The root never changes.** A handle, an app address, an account type, a name on a chain, a state of residence or a role in an entity is not a substitute for it.
- **Accounts stay separate.** Using the same handle in several accounts does not merge ownership, wallets, credentials, authority or data.
- **Each jurisdiction is evidenced on its own.** A personal account in one state and an organization formed in another are different bindings. One is never used as proof of the other.
- **Acting for an entity needs both paths:** evidence about the entity, and current evidence that the person holds a role in it.

## An example (names are illustrative)

```text
Your name — M5HUM / TCID (persistent; kept private)
  ├─ IAM → your M5POD
  ├─ handle: yourname
  ├─ Bank of Me — personal context
  │    └─ United States → California → Los Angeles
  └─ Bank of Us — a co-op formed in Wyoming
       └─ United States → Wyoming
            └─ the co-op's own registration evidence and credential
```

## Moving and renaming

If you move, your personal residence binding is updated after new evidence, but your origin record, your TCID and any entity's state of formation do not change. If you choose a new handle, the old one stays in your history and is not reissued to someone else. History is append-only and stays in your M5POD; public lookups do not reveal where you lived before.

:::note
Example names on this page are illustrative. A name is live only once its registration and controller are confirmed.
:::

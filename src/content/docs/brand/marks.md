## The Foundation Star

The issuer of sovereign access rights. **Five petals, five sovereign access rights**; the ringed core is the certificate of title that holds them together.

| Petal | Right |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Run open models |
| 2 | Own your compute |
| 3 | Prove who you are |
| 4 | Take your records with you |
| 5 | Connect any tool |

A work carrying all five rights shows the full star. A partial star shows exactly which rights are granted, so anyone can see at a glance what they really own.

## The family: one star, two marks beneath it

| Mark | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **The Star** | Issues rights. TitleChain Foundation certifies the title and the rights attached to it |
| **M5POD** | Runs them sovereign. The pod you control; its gold node is the star's core, linking it back to the title |
| **The Link** | Declares provenance: how the work was made |

### Provenance

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One link | Human-made |
| Two links | AI-assisted, human-directed |
| Filled link | AI-generated, human-reviewed |

## The credit line

The star always leads:

```text
TITLECHAIN FOUNDATION · M5POD · AI-ASSISTED, HUMAN-DIRECTED · © 2026 · TITLE [CERTIFICATE ID]
```

Replace the provenance and the certificate ID with your own. The member kit of images for embedding these marks and notices in your own work is in preparation.

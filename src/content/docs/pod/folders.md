Your M5POD downloads as one ZIP, already organized:

```
M5POD/
  Open M5POD.html     your dashboard; opens in any browser and sends nothing anywhere
  m5readme.md         map of the whole pod
  M0-M5IAM-000/       your free IAM: attestation, identifiers, registry, certificate, ledger
  M1-M5BKOM-001/      Bank of Me: ten vaults for your life
  M5-CV/              your roles and credentials
  M2-M5BKOU-002/      Bank of Us: your co-op, family or group
  M3-M5BKOB-003/      Bank of Business: your business, from product to back office
  brain/              your notes; open in any notes app
  m5agent/            rules for any agent you choose
```

Only your IAM is active. The other accounts are **ready, not activated**: you can fill their folders now, and the accounts activate later.

## Conventions

- Every folder starts with **m5readme.md**, which says what belongs there, its privacy level and the typical file types.
- Agent tooling lives in **m5agent/**. Nothing is tied to one agent or vendor.
- Folders follow the Solid pod model: each is a container you can grant or withhold.

See [What goes in each folder](/M5-IAM-docs/pod/file-types/).
